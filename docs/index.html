Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 14, 2026

AleClip ("the App") is developed by themanoftalent. We respect and protect your privacy above all else.

1. Zero Data Collection

  • We do not collect, track, transmit, or share any personal data, clipboard contents, or device identifiers.
  • We do not include any third-party analytics frameworks, crash collectors, or advertising SDKs.
  • Our App conforms strictly with Apple's PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy specification declaring zero collected data types.

2. On-Device Local Storage

All clips, notes, colors, and user preferences are stored strictly inside your device's local sandbox or shared App Group container. Nothing is ever uploaded to external servers.

3. Clipboard Access

Clipboard access is only invoked to capture and copy clips at the user's direction. Clipboard data never leaves your device.

4. Data Deletion & Control

You have complete control over your history. You can remove individual clips, empty the Trash folder, or uninstall the app to immediately delete all data permanently.
