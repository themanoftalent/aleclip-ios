# Privacy Policy for AleClip

**Effective Date:** October 14, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2026  

AleClip ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed by **themanoftalent**. Your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your data when you use AleClip for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
AleClip is built on a **zero-telemetry, zero-collection** architecture:
- We **do not collect**, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, identifiers, or clipboard content.
- We **do not track** your usage patterns, browsing habits, device identifiers, IP addresses, or location.
- We **do not integrate** any third-party analytics SDKs, crash trackers, or advertising frameworks (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, or Flurry).

---

### 2. On-Device Storage & Local Processing
- All clipboard clips, notes, images, colors, and user preferences are stored exclusively on your local device within the secure sandboxed container or App Group (`group.com.themanoftalent.aleclip`).
- No data is transmitted across the internet to external servers.
- Data remains under your full control at all times. If you delete a clip or delete the application, the corresponding local files are permanently erased from your device.

---

### 3. Clipboard Access
- iOS requires user permission or active app foreground status to access the system pasteboard (`UIPasteboard`).
- AleClip only inspects the pasteboard to save snippets into your personal on-device history when the app is active or when you explicitly tap paste or share via the Share Sheet extension.
- Pasteboard content is never shared with third parties or external services.

---

### 4. Backup & Export
- If you choose to export your clipboard history using the built-in JSON backup tool, the exported file is saved locally to your device or shared via standard iOS share sheets strictly according to your explicit action.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
Because AleClip does not collect or transmit any personal data, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The App is safe for users of all ages.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
