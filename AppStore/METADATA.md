## App Description (English)

```text
AleClip is the modern, colorful, and privacy-first clipboard companion crafted for iPhone and iPad. Designed for speed, convenience, and peace of mind, AleClip helps you organize and manage your snippets, links, and notes—without ever sending a single byte of your data to external servers.

KEY FEATURES:

• INSTANT IN-APP & ON-OPEN CAPTURE
Quickly save text, links, color hex codes, or images upon opening the app or with the dedicated one-tap Paste action.

• ACCIDENTAL DELETE PROTECTION & TRASH RECOVERY
Never fear losing an important note or snippet again! AleClip features instant Undo (⌘Z / floating toast) and a dedicated Trash folder. Accidentally deleted clips can be restored with a single tap.

• NATIVE SHARE EXTENSION
Save links, articles, and text directly from Safari, Notes, Photos, or any other app using the native iOS Share Sheet—no switching required.

• PIN ESSENTIAL CLIPS
Keep frequent links, code snippets, email templates, and important memos pinned to the top of your list for instant one-tap copying.

• INTELLIGENT SEARCH & TYPE FILTERING
Find exactly what you need in milliseconds. Filter by Links, Text, Images, Files, Colors, or Pinned items with a clean, responsive swipeable tab bar.

• VIBRANT SPECTRUM DESIGN (ZERO PINK)
Enjoy a radiant, eye-friendly spectrum gradient palette engineered across Electric Cyan, Cobalt Azure, Neon Mint, Emerald Green, Electric Lime, and Sunburst Gold. Fully optimized for both Dark Mode and Light Mode with fluid iOS haptic feedback.

• 100% LOCAL & PRIVATE (ZERO TELEMETRY)
Your clipboard is personal. AleClip contains zero analytics, zero trackers, zero advertisements, and zero third-party servers. All data stays strictly on your local device.

• SEAMLESS BACKUP & EXPORT
Easily backup your clipboard history or export to JSON to migrate between devices whenever you want.

Upgrade your daily workflow with AleClip—the clipboard companion you can trust.
```
